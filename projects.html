AI Curriculum for TDRA Summer Camp 2026

MolKet SAS is proud to have assisted OTIMI Systems Company LLC in delivering the Artificial Intelligence curriculum for TDRA Summer Camp 2026, organised by the UAE's Telecommunications and Digital Government Regulatory Authority (TDRA). OTIMI provided these services to Global Innovation & Entrepreneurship (GIE) and MENA Robotics, the programme's implementation partners.

Through management consulting services, MolKet supported OTIMI with specialist expertise in AI curriculum design, content development and trainer preparation for the AI Innovators track — a hands-on programme introducing students aged 9 to 15 to applied artificial intelligence, data quality and responsible technology use in Abu Dhabi and Dubai.

Congratulations to TDRA for its commitment to youth empowerment, and our thanks to OTIMI Systems, GIE and MENA Robotics for a valued collaboration. This engagement is part of MolKet's growing education and training practice, alongside our work in quantum computing.

Research

SQD-PLS: Quantum Linear Solver

Extends sample-based quantum diagonalization from chemistry to linear systems A x = b. The state |b⟩ is evolved under A on a quantum computer, and the sampled bit strings select a small subspace where the system is solved classically, with no phase estimation, ancilla qubits or post-selection (unlike HHL). A 4×4 system was solved exactly on IBM's ibm_fez, and a 1024-unknown problem reached 10⁻⁵ relative error with 33 configurations in simulation.

Validating SQD: Properties, Spin and Controls

Asks whether sample-based quantum diagonalization (SQD) results can be judged by more than the energy. Using archived IBM hardware data for N₂ and the 2025 77-qubit iron–sulfur cluster calculation, Alain Chancé tracks density properties and spin against converged references: properties stop improving while the energy still falls, and random or coupled-cluster-seeded baselines can match hardware results at equal subspace size. The paper recommends reporting a density property and spin alongside the energy.
