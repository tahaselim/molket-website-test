News & Events

Talks, panels and publications from the MolKet team

June 2026Recognition

Vice-Chair of IEEE P1947

Congratulations to Alain Chancé on his appointment as Vice-Chair of the IEEE P1947 working group, and thank you for his ongoing contributions to the project. This is a significant recognition of Alain's expertise and dedication to advancing quantum computing standards.

May 2026Course

New Quantum Computing Course

Dr. Taha Selim published a new course on quantum computing from scratch.

April 2026Event

Cairo University Spring School in Quantum Technologies

Cairo University hosted a spring school in quantum technologies, with Dr. Taha Selim as a co-organizer and speaker. The event attracted students and researchers from across the region and featured a panel discussion on advanced quantum algorithms by Dr. Keeper L. Sharkey and Alain Chancé.

2025Panel

QED-C Quantum & AI Use Cases Panel

Dr. Taha Selim and Alain Chancé joined the QED-C panel on use cases at the intersection of quantum computing and AI.

July 2024Talk

Simulating quantum molecular dynamics with Julia at JuliaCon 2024

Our founders co-presented a lightning talk on simulating quantum molecular dynamics with Julia and MolKet.jl.

March 2024Talk

CO₂ as a quantum molecular sensor at the APS March Meeting 2024

Dr. Taha Selim presented research on carbon dioxide as a quantum molecular sensor in protoplanetary disks.

February 2024Talk

Keynote at the World AI Cannes Festival (WAICF)

Alain Chancé co-delivered the keynote "Combining the power of AI with Quantum" in Cannes, France.

2023Publication

New paper in The Journal of Chemical Physics

State-to-state rovibrational transition rates for CO₂ in the bend mode in collisions with He atoms, J. Chem. Phys. 159, 164310 (2023).

MolKet offers consulting and AI services for modeling quantum molecular solutions with cloud-based software on hybrid HPC and quantum computing platforms.
